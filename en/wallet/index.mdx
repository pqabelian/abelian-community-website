# Your assets. Your control.

The Abelian Pro wallet is your all-in-one home for digital assets. You can hold ABEL alongside other currencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum, and swap between them right in the app, with no need to jump between platforms. When you send ABEL, you choose how private each transaction is with Multi-Layer Privacy: Transparent for quick, low-cost public transfers, or Shielded to keep the details confidential. It's protected by post-quantum cryptography, and a simple recovery phrase means your funds are always in your hands.

Abelian Improvement Proposals (AIPs) are structured documents for proposing, discussing, and documenting improvements to the Abelian blockchain ecosystem. They describe a specific change or issue, explain its motivation, and provide technical details that help the community review and implement it.

## What AIPs cover

The repository organizes proposals into three types:

- **Standards Track** proposals describe technical standards or changes that may affect network behavior or compatibility.
- **Informational** proposals document information or guidance for the Abelian ecosystem.
- **Process** proposals describe processes and procedures used by the project or community.

Each proposal includes a status, such as Draft, Proposed, Final, Active, Rejected, Deprecated, or Superseded. A proposal's status indicates its stage or standing in the AIP process; acceptance alone does not make it a network standard. Adoption depends on consensus among the Abelian community and users.

## Read and discuss AIPs

Browse proposal documents, status information, and contribution guidance in the [Abelian AIPs repository](https://github.com/pqabelian/aips). To suggest a proposal or participate in discussion, use the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/pqabelian/aips/issues).

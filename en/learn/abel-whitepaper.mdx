## Introduction

The Abelian whitepaper lays out the thinking behind the Abelian network: how to build a cryptocurrency that protects both users’ privacy and their funds against future quantum attacks. Dated **18 February 2022**, it credits its authors under the pseudonyms **Alice, Bob, Eve, and λ**.

It brings together lattice-based cryptography, linkable ring signatures, and zero-knowledge proofs to help hide transaction participants and amounts, while proposing optional accountability for situations where oversight is needed. The paper served as Abelian’s original technical blueprint, explaining how its security, privacy options, transactions, and Proof-of-Work network would fit together. It describes the foundations of Abelian, rather than every feature available today.

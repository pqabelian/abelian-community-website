## Payments

Multi-asset mobile wallet with BTC, ETH, USDT and USDC alongside ABEL. Pseudonymous addresses for exchange and merchant rails, fully private addresses for everything else.

## Stablecoins

Issue on the AUT standard and the token inherits the chain's privacy tiers and quantum resistance. A settlement asset that does not need a migration plan written into its prospectus.

## Layer 2 and smart contracts

QDay Network is an EVM-compatible L2 that inherits post-quantum settlement from Abelian. Deploy with the Solidity toolchain you already use, and settle on cryptography that outlives it.

## Real-world assets

Tokenised bonds, funds and title records with ten to thirty year lifetimes. Confidential by default, disclosable to an auditor through a view key, and cryptographically valid past the point where ECDSA is disallowed.

## Institutional custody and compliance

Partial view keys let a custodian or regulator verify balances and flows without gaining spend authority. Privacy that survives a compliance review.
